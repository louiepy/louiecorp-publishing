The publication

About LouieCorp

LouieCorp is an independent newsroom covering public life — politics, business, technology, Africa, world affairs, society and culture — with care for the record and respect for the reader.

We report on the exercise of power, the conduct of institutions, and the decisions that shape communities. The newsroom publishes news, analysis and commentary for readers who want more than a fragment of the day: work that has been reported, edited and argued with professional care.

Independence

Independence here is a method. Reporting is distinguished from opinion. Claims are tested against the public record. Sources are handled with care. When we get something wrong, we correct it. The masthead is not aligned to a party, government or commercial interest.

The desks

Our desks cover Politics, Business, Technology, Africa, World, Society, Culture, Opinion and Investigations. The aim is not to exhaust a subject in a single sitting, but to give readers a clear account of what happened, why it matters, and what remains in dispute.
